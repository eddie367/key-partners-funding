How It Works

From Application
to Certificate of Occupancy.

The same proven 6-step process as our rehab loans — built for the longer timeline and milestone-based nature of ground-up construction.

What We Underwrite
  • The land / lot value
  • Your construction budget and scope
  • Completed value (ARV / appraised value after build)
  • Your experience as a builder or developer
  • Credit (660 minimum FICO)
01
Apply
Submit our application or request a quote. Include your lot address, project scope, construction budget, and expected completed value. Our team will begin reviewing right away.
02
Process
Once we confirm it's a good fit, we'll request additional information to begin underwriting — plans, permits if available, builder credentials, and comparable sales.
03
Underwrite
We confirm the deal economics — lot value, construction costs, and projected completed value — and review your scope of work to align our estimates with yours.
04
Fund
Tell us your start date and we'll have loan documents ready. Initial advance covers land acquisition and early-stage costs. You break ground.
05
Construction Draws
Interest-only monthly payments on the drawn balance. As you hit construction milestones, we release additional draw funds — keeping pace with your project.
06
Repay
Once the project is complete and you sell or refinance into permanent financing, we issue a payoff to your title company. No prepayment penalty — you keep all the upside.
