Compare DSCR rental math across states and cities: median rent, home value, property tax rate and gross yield, with a page of worked numbers for each market.

Key Partners Funding4 min readInvestors comparing rental markets

A DSCR loan qualifies on the rent, so where you buy decides how easy it is to qualify. Markets where rent is high relative to home prices clear a 1.00 ratio at typical prices. Markets where homes are expensive relative to rent need a lower price, a stronger rent or a larger down payment. Where we lend

We make DSCR loans on long term rentals in 42 states plus Washington DC, everywhere except Minnesota, Nevada, New Jersey, New York, North Dakota, Pennsylvania, South Dakota and Utah. Every state we serve has a page above.

  • Up to 80% of the property's value
  • Qualifies on the rent, with no tax returns, W-2s or pay stubs
  • 620 minimum credit score
  • Single family and 2 to 4 unit rentals on long term leases
  • Closes in an LLC
  • 30 year fixed options and step down prepayment choices
  • Closings in about 21 to 30 days once conditions are met

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2024 5 year estimates. Tax rate is the owner occupied median tax divided by median value; rentals often pay more.
