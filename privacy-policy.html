Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Key Partners Funding ("Key Partners Funding," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit keypartnersfunding.com or interact with our services. By using this site or submitting information through our forms, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you fill out a loan inquiry, request a Proof of Funds letter, or otherwise contact us, you may provide information such as:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Property address and details about the transaction
  • Purchase price, loan amount, projected rents, and other financial details related to the property
  • Entity information (LLC name, formation documents) when applicable
  • Approximate credit score and any additional notes you choose to share

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the site, we may automatically collect technical information including IP address, browser type, device information, referring URLs, pages viewed, and timestamps. This information is collected through standard server logs and analytics tools.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Evaluate and respond to loan inquiries and Proof of Funds requests
  • Communicate with you about your inquiry, including by phone, email, or text
  • Underwrite, process, and service loans
  • Improve our website, services, and marketing
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized use of our services

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Capital partners, investors, and underwriters who participate in funding loans
  • Service providers who support our business — including hosting, email delivery, customer relationship management, and analytics providers
  • Verification services such as title companies, appraisers, credit reporting agencies, and background check vendors when relevant to processing your loan
  • Legal and regulatory authorities when required by law, subpoena, or to protect our rights or those of others
  • Successors in the event of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of business assets

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure traffic, and improve performance. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not work properly as a result.

5. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions in our emails or by contacting us directly. You may also request that we update or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping obligations.

7. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed to individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will delete it.

8. Third-Party Links

Our site may include links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties, and we encourage you to review their policies before providing any information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated through other means.
