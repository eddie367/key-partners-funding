How It Works

Simple Process.
Fast Close.

Our DSCR process is designed to be fast and light on documentation — because we underwrite the asset, not your personal financial history.

Light Doc Checklist
  • Government ID
  • Lease (or market rent analysis)
  • LLC entity formation docs (required — all loans close in an LLC)
  • Property insurance quote
  • That's it — no W-2s, tax returns, or pay stubs

The rental income of the property is what qualifies the loan, not your personal income history. This makes DSCR ideal for self-employed investors, those with complex tax returns, or investors with multiple write-offs.
We require a minimum 620 FICO score for DSCR loans. This is lower than our rehab and construction products (which require 660) because the underwriting focus is primarily on the property's income — your credit still matters, but it's weighted differently.
We lend on 1–4 unit residential investment properties: single-family rentals, duplexes, triplexes, and quadplexes. The property must be leased (or have market rent support) on a long-term basis. Not eligible: commercial properties, 5+ unit multifamily, mixed-use, owner-occupied homes, and short-term rentals (Airbnb, VRBO, or any rental under 30 days). We need a standard long-term lease — short-term and vacation rental income does not qualify under our DSCR program.
Yes — unlike our rehab and construction loans, DSCR loans do have a prepayment penalty. We offer step-down prepayment options: 5-year, 3-year, or 1-year step down. This means if you sell or refinance during the prepayment period, a declining fee applies. We'll walk you through the options when we discuss your loan terms.
Yes — all DSCR loans must close in the name of an LLC. We do not lend to individuals on this program. Most serious investors prefer LLC vesting anyway for liability protection and clean accounting. If you don't have an entity yet, your attorney or a service like LegalZoom can typically set one up in a few business days. We'll need your formation documents (Articles of Organization, Operating Agreement, EIN letter) before closing.
We can close DSCR loans in as little as 21–30 days once all conditions are satisfied. Typical timeline is a bit longer depending on the appraisal and title, but our process is designed to move quickly so you don't lose time-sensitive deals.
